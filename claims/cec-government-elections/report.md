# Are the Chief Election Commissioner and the Union government working together to make India's elections unfair?

As of 2026-10-18 · version 2 · artifact `bf4bff4f5367`

> Heard as: "CEC and modi government, together are trying to rig Indian democracy"
> "Rig" is replaced with "make elections unfair" so the question reads the same to both sides. The claim is then split into parts that can each be checked: how the referee is appointed, what happened to voter rolls, voting machines, rule enforcement, transparency, how the Commission works inside, and finally intent.

This is a map of the evidence, not a verdict on any person or party. Each part of the claim is checked on its own, and the parts are never added up into one answer.

## What is settled

- **K1. Under the 2023 law, the government side holds a majority on the panel that picks Election Commissioners.** Supported. The supporting case holds up, and no challenge to it stands.
- **K3. Bihar's 2025 roll revision (SIR) removed tens of lakhs of names.** Supported. The supporting case holds up, and no challenge to it stands.
- **K5. There was an organised attempt to fraudulently delete voters in Aland, Karnataka.** Supported. The supporting case holds up, and no challenge to it stands.
- **K8. Voting machines (EVMs) have been tampered with to change results.** Challenged. A challenge holds up, and the supporting case does not. This depends on a judgment call: "The Supreme Court examined it and made no finding of tampering" was judged stronger than "Checks are too thin to catch tampering". It rests on one argument: "The Supreme Court examined it and made no finding of tampering".
- **K10. Since late 2024, public access to election records and polling footage has been narrowed.** Supported. The supporting case holds up, and no challenge to it stands.

## What is open, and what would settle it

- **K2. The 2023 law made the Election Commission less independent than before.** Contested. The two sides block each other; it is open.
  - Hinges on which is stronger: "Before 2023 the government chose alone" (then this part is challenged) or "The law removed the member from outside government" (then it is supported).
  - *What would settle it:* The Supreme Court's ruling on the 2023 Act, which is still pending; and agreeing which 'before' to compare with.
- **K4. The Bihar SIR left out many eligible voters.** Contested. The two sides block each other; it is open.
  - Hinges on which is stronger: "The court upheld it, and the ECI says no one appealed" (then this part is challenged) or "The scale and the paperwork put eligible voters at risk" (then it is supported).
  - *What would settle it:* A field check of a random sample of deleted names, showing what share were wrongly removed.
- **K6. The ECI held back data that investigators needed in the Aland case.** Contested. The two sides block each other; it is open.
  - Hinges on which is stronger: "The ECI says it gave everything and started the case" (then this part is challenged) or "Investigators say their requests went unanswered" (then it is supported).
  - *What would settle it:* The list of what the Karnataka CID asked for and what the ECI handed over, made public or placed before a court.
- **K7. Voter rolls in Mahadevapura and Maharashtra were padded with fake or duplicate entries.** Contested. The two sides block each other; it is open.
  - Hinges on which is stronger: "The ECI's numbers, and no sworn complaint" (then this part is challenged) or "The opposition's analysis of the rolls" (then it is supported).
  - *What would settle it:* The named entries submitted under the statutory declaration and checked one by one, plus an independent duplicate check of machine-readable rolls.
- **K9. The ECI enforces the Model Code of Conduct more softly against the ruling party.** Contested. The two sides block each other; it is open.
  - Hinges on which is stronger: "Both parties got the same treatment" (then this part is challenged) or "The PM's speech drew a notice to his party, not to him" (then it is supported).
  - *What would settle it:* A full log of Model Code complaints, with party, action taken, and days taken, analysed independently.
- **K11. Inside the ECI, the Chief Election Commissioner has taken decisions over the objections of the other two commissioners.** Contested. The two sides block each other; it is open.
  - Hinges on which is stronger: "The ECI says its decisions were unanimous" (then this part is challenged) or "Fourteen dissent notes on record" (then it is supported).
  - *What would settle it:* The commission's minutes and the dissent notes, placed before Parliament or a court.
- **K12. The CEC and the Union government are acting together to tilt elections toward the ruling party.** Unproven either way. Neither side's case survives the challenges against it.
  - *What would settle it:* Direct evidence of coordination, which is not public. Patterns alone cannot settle intent; the parts above, once settled, would narrow it.

## What each side weighs

These are values, not facts. They show where people differ in what they care about, not who is right.

- The referee must be seen to be independent, not only be independent. *(stressed by critics of the 2023 law, echoed from the bench in July 2026)*
- An elected Parliament makes the law on appointments, and constitutional offices should not be presumed to act in bad faith. *(stressed by the Union government)*
- Clean rolls: no dead, shifted, duplicate or ineligible names. *(stressed by the ECI)*
- No eligible voter left off, and the burden of proof on the state rather than the voter. *(stressed by petitioners and opposition parties)*
- Election records open enough that anyone can check them. *(stressed by RTI activists and opposition parties)*
- Voter privacy, and stopping selective clips being used to mislead. *(stressed by the ECI)*
- Trust in a working system, without unwarranted suspicion. *(stressed by Justice Datta, in the 2024 VVPAT judgment)*

## Steps both sides could take

- **Field-check a random sample of names deleted in the Bihar SIR and publish what share were wrongly removed.** Who: the ECI with an independent body, or a court-appointed panel. Helps settle K4. Both sides say they want accurate rolls; a sample tests accuracy without redoing the revision.
- **Submit the named Mahadevapura entries through the sworn declaration, and release machine-readable rolls with privacy safeguards for an independent duplicate check.** Who: Congress and the ECI. Helps settle K7. The ECI asked for named entries and the opposition asked for searchable rolls; doing both lets each entry be checked.
- **Publish the list of what the Karnataka CID asked the ECI for on Aland, and what was handed over.** Who: the ECI and the Karnataka CID, or a court. Helps settle K6. Both say they want the Aland culprits caught; the list ends the dispute about who held what back.
- **Publish a log of every Model Code complaint: date, party, speaker, action taken, days taken.** Who: the ECI, with independent researchers analysing it. Helps settle K9. If enforcement is even, the log shows it; if not, it shows where.
- **Place the commission's minutes and the dissent notes on record.** Who: the ECI, before Parliament or a court. Helps settle K11. The ECI says its decisions were unanimous; the record would show it.
- **Run burnt-memory checks wherever candidates ask, and pilot a larger VVPAT sample in a few constituencies.** Who: the ECI and candidates. Helps settle K8. Cheap ways to raise confidence in the machines, whichever side doubts them.
- **Agree in advance to treat the Supreme Court's ruling on the 2023 Act as settling its legal side.** Who: the parties to the case. Helps settle K2. Both sides have gone to the same court; agreeing to its ruling turns a shouting match into a wait.
- **Check your own and your family's names on the voter roll and file a correction if anything is wrong.** Who: any voter, at voters.eci.gov.in. Helps settle K4, K7. Every voter can do this today, whichever side they are on, and every correction makes the rolls more accurate.

## Each part in detail

### K1. Under the 2023 law, the government side holds a majority on the panel that picks Election Commissioners.

*Fact · supported*

Also heard as: "The government picks its own referee"

**For**

- **Two of three panel members come from the government** (stands). The panel is the Prime Minister, a Cabinet Minister he nominates, and the Leader of the Opposition, so the government side has two votes of three.
  - The 2023 Act set up a three-member selection committee: the Prime Minister, a Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the Prime Minister, and the Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha. ([Deccan Herald, 2026-07-30](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/should-there-not-be-a-show-of-fairness-sc-questions-exclusion-of-cji-from-cec-ecs-appointment-panel-4093352))

### K2. The 2023 law made the Election Commission less independent than before.

*Meaning · contested*

**For**

- **The law removed the member from outside government** (open). Compared with the court's 2023 arrangement, which put the Chief Justice on the panel, the law swapped the only member from outside the executive and legislature for a minister, so the government can now pick commissioners on its own votes.
  - The Act replaced the arrangement set out in the Anoop Baranwal judgment, under which the Chief Justice of India sat on the panel, with a Cabinet Minister. ([Deccan Herald, 2026-07-30](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/should-there-not-be-a-show-of-fairness-sc-questions-exclusion-of-cji-from-cec-ecs-appointment-panel-4093352))
  - Hearing the challenge in July 2026, Justice Dipankar Datta asked: "Election Commissioner has to be an independent person. Should there not be a show of fairness?" ([Deccan Herald, 2026-07-30](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/should-there-not-be-a-show-of-fairness-sc-questions-exclusion-of-cji-from-cec-ecs-appointment-panel-4093352))

**Against**

- **Before 2023 the government chose alone** (open). Compared with how commissioners were chosen for decades, by the government alone, the law adds the Leader of the Opposition, so it is not less independent than before; the government argues the court's arrangement was only meant to last until Parliament made a law.
  - The ECI said commissioners were previously appointed by prime ministers, that since 2023 they are appointed under a law for the first time, and that "obviously the new system is better". ([The Tribune, 2025-06-08](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/ec-rejects-maha-election-rigging-charge-as-absurd-dont-evade-counters-rahul/amp/?utm=relatedarticles))
  - The Attorney General and Solicitor General argued the Anoop Baranwal judgment was only an interim arrangement and did not limit Parliament's power under Article 324. ([Deccan Herald, 2026-07-30](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/should-there-not-be-a-show-of-fairness-sc-questions-exclusion-of-cji-from-cec-ecs-appointment-panel-4093352))

**How they meet**

- "Before 2023 the government chose alone" rebuts "The law removed the member from outside government": the two compare against different starting points
- "The law removed the member from outside government" rebuts "Before 2023 the government chose alone": the two compare against different starting points

### K3. Bihar's 2025 roll revision (SIR) removed tens of lakhs of names.

*Fact · supported*

**For**

- **65 lakh off the draft, 47 lakh fewer at the end** (stands). The ECI's own draft and final rolls show the scale of removals.
  - About 65 lakh names were left out of the draft roll published on 1 August 2025; the Supreme Court had the ECI post the deleted names with reasons. ([The Tribune (PTI), 2025-08-17](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/names-of-65-lakh-voters-deleted-from-bihar-sir-posted-on-district-websites/amp), [ETV Bharat, 2025-10-07](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/cant-have-a-roving-inquiry-those-aggrieved-should-file-affidavits-sc-on-bihar-sir-enn25100705353))
  - The final roll of 30 September 2025 had about 7.42 crore electors, against about 7.89 crore before the revision, a net drop of about 47 lakh, after 21.53 lakh were added and 3.66 lakh more removed. ([SabrangIndia, 2025-10-01](https://sabrangindia.in/bihar-sir-final-voter-list-sees-47-lakh-names-removed-total-electorate-drops-to-7-42-crore), [ETV Bharat, 2025-10-07](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/cant-have-a-roving-inquiry-those-aggrieved-should-file-affidavits-sc-on-bihar-sir-enn25100705353))

### K4. The Bihar SIR left out many eligible voters.

*Meaning · contested*

Also heard as: "SIR is mass disenfranchisement"

**For**

- **The scale and the paperwork put eligible voters at risk** (open). When tens of lakhs are removed and people missing from the 2002 roll must prove links to it, some eligible voters are likely to fall out; the court itself had to add safeguards along the way.
  - Petitioners challenged the requirement that voters absent from the 2002 rolls prove a link to someone on those rolls. ([The News Minute, 2026-05-27](https://www.thenewsminute.com/india/supreme-court-upholds-validity-of-sir-says-eci-can-examine-citizenship))
  - During the hearings the court had the ECI publish the deleted names with reasons and, in September 2025, accept Aadhaar as a 12th document for identity. ([The Tribune (PTI), 2025-08-17](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/names-of-65-lakh-voters-deleted-from-bihar-sir-posted-on-district-websites/amp), [LiveLaw, 2025-09-08](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-directs-eci-to-accept-aadhaar-card-as-12th-document-in-bihar-sir-as-proof-of-identity-303182))
  - ADR's counsel told the court the deletions reflect a systemic problem and cited a person deleted in the draft who applied for inclusion but was not restored. ([ETV Bharat, 2025-10-07](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/cant-have-a-roving-inquiry-those-aggrieved-should-file-affidavits-sc-on-bihar-sir-enn25100705353))

**Against**

- **The court upheld it, and the ECI says no one appealed** (open). The Supreme Court found the SIR lawful with safeguards, the state's election office gives deaths, migration and duplicates as the reasons, and the ECI said no deleted voter had appealed.
  - On 27 May 2026 the Supreme Court upheld the ECI's power to conduct the SIR, including examining citizenship for inclusion on the rolls, and said it had not acted in excess of its statutory powers, with a presumption in favour of electors already on the rolls. ([The News Minute, 2026-05-27](https://www.thenewsminute.com/india/supreme-court-upholds-validity-of-sir-says-eci-can-examine-citizenship), [Outlook, 2026-09-23](https://www.outlookindia.com/national/sir-to-removal-bid-why-gyanesh-kumars-eci-tenure-has-faced-repeated-scrutiny))
  - In October 2025 the ECI's counsel told the court that no deleted voter had filed an appeal or complaint with it; the court said it could not hold a roving inquiry and asked for affidavits from those aggrieved, while ADR said it could produce 100. ([ETV Bharat, 2025-10-07](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/cant-have-a-roving-inquiry-those-aggrieved-should-file-affidavits-sc-on-bihar-sir-enn25100705353))
  - The Bihar Chief Electoral Officer's office attributed draft-stage deletions to deaths (about 22 lakh), migration or absence (about 36 lakh) and duplicates (about 7 lakh). ([SabrangIndia, 2025-10-01](https://sabrangindia.in/bihar-sir-final-voter-list-sees-47-lakh-names-removed-total-electorate-drops-to-7-42-crore))

**How they meet**

- "The court upheld it, and the ECI says no one appealed" rebuts "The scale and the paperwork put eligible voters at risk": lawful with safeguards and few appeals, against scale and burden
- "The scale and the paperwork put eligible voters at risk" rebuts "The court upheld it, and the ECI says no one appealed": a lawful process can still drop eligible people; few appeals may reflect the burden of appealing

### K5. There was an organised attempt to fraudulently delete voters in Aland, Karnataka.

*Fact · supported*

**For**

- **A chargesheet and the ECI's own numbers** (stands). Police have charged named people for a paid scheme of fake deletion requests, and the ECI's figures show thousands of such requests.
  - The Karnataka CID's SIT chargesheet names a former BJP MLA and his son as prime accused in an alleged conspiracy to file 5,994 fraudulent deletion applications between December 2022 and February 2023. ([The News Minute, 2025-12-13](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/aland-vote-fraud-ex-bjp-mla-and-son-prime-accused-in-chargesheet), [Business Standard, 2025-12-13](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/karnataka-vote-theft-case-sit-names-ex-bjp-mla-son-in-chargesheet-125121300207_1.html))
  - An ECI press release of 19 September 2025 said it received 6,018 online deletion applications for Aland, rejected 5,994 as incorrect and approved 24; the ECI filed a police complaint on 21 February 2023 after a complaint from MLA B R Patil. ([The News Minute, 2025-12-13](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/aland-vote-fraud-ex-bjp-mla-and-son-prime-accused-in-chargesheet))

### K6. The ECI held back data that investigators needed in the Aland case.

*Meaning · contested*

Also heard as: "The ECI is shielding vote thieves"

**For**

- **Investigators say their requests went unanswered** (open). If the CID asked repeatedly for technical data and did not get it, the probe was held back.
  - Reporting on the chargesheet says the CID wrote 18 letters asking the ECI for technical data and that the ECI did not share it. ([The News Minute, 2025-12-13](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/aland-vote-fraud-ex-bjp-mla-and-son-prime-accused-in-chargesheet))

**Against**

- **The ECI says it gave everything and started the case** (open). The ECI itself registered the first complaint and says it handed over all available information, which does not fit a body holding back data.
  - The ECI said the case was registered by its own authority in 2023 and that it handed over all available information to the Karnataka CID. ([Deccan Herald, 2025-09-19](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/ec-hits-back-at-rahul-gandhi-says-nothing-withheld-in-aland-fake-voter-deletion-probe-3736331))

**How they meet**

- "The ECI says it gave everything and started the case" rebuts "Investigators say their requests went unanswered": the two accounts of the same correspondence conflict
- "Investigators say their requests went unanswered" rebuts "The ECI says it gave everything and started the case": the two accounts of the same correspondence conflict

### K7. Voter rolls in Mahadevapura and Maharashtra were padded with fake or duplicate entries.

*Meaning · contested*

Also heard as: "Vote chori"

**For**

- **The opposition's analysis of the rolls** (open). If one seat has over a lakh suspect entries and, on Congress's figures, a state has more voters than its projected adult population, that suggests the rolls were padded.
  - Rahul Gandhi said a Congress research team found over one lakh fake votes in Mahadevapura out of about 6.5 lakh, in four categories: fake names, duplicates, invalid addresses and bulk additions. ([Business Today, 2025-08-07](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/over-1l-votes-stolen-in-mahadevapura-assembly-rahul-gandhis-big-claim-against-ec-poll-body-responds-488311-2025-08-07))
  - He said Maharashtra had 9.7 crore voters against an adult population of 9.54 crore, with about 39 lakh added between the 2024 Lok Sabha and assembly polls. ([Business Standard, 2025-02-07](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/rahul-gandhi-maharashtra-voters-population-claim-election-commission-reply-125020700833_1.html))

**Against**

- **The ECI's numbers, and no sworn complaint** (open). On EC figures, Maharashtra's growth was lower than in some other states, few appeals were filed, and the claims have not been submitted in the sworn form that would let officials check each name.
  - EC sources said Maharashtra's electors rose 8.14% between the 2019 and 2024 assembly polls, less than Jharkhand (13.40%) or Telangana (16.16%) over their own cycles, and only 89 first appeals were filed after the rolls were finalised. ([The Tribune, 2025-06-08](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/ec-rejects-maha-election-rigging-charge-as-absurd-dont-evade-counters-rahul/amp/?utm=relatedarticles))
  - Karnataka's Chief Electoral Officer asked Rahul Gandhi to sign a declaration under Rule 20(3)(b) naming the electors, and the CEC said he must give an affidavit within seven days or apologise, failing which the allegations would be treated as baseless. ([Business Today, 2025-08-07](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/over-1l-votes-stolen-in-mahadevapura-assembly-rahul-gandhis-big-claim-against-ec-poll-body-responds-488311-2025-08-07), [The Sentinel, 2025-08-18](https://www.sentinelassam.com/topheadlines/give-affidavit-or-apologise-no-third-option-cec-to-rahul-gandhi))

**How they meet**

- "The ECI's numbers, and no sworn complaint" rebuts "The opposition's analysis of the rolls": official totals and the missing sworn complaint, against a party's own entry-level analysis
- "The opposition's analysis of the rolls" rebuts "The ECI's numbers, and no sworn complaint": state-level growth rates do not test whether particular entries are fake

### K8. Voting machines (EVMs) have been tampered with to change results.

*Meaning · challenged*

Also heard as: "EVMs are hacked"

**For**

- **Checks are too thin to catch tampering** (falls). Petitioners alleged data discrepancies and argued that checking paper slips in only five polling stations per seat leaves tampering possible.
  - ADR alleged tampering and data discrepancies and argued that VVPAT checks in only five randomly selected polling stations per constituency were insufficient. ([Supreme Court Observer, 2024-04-26](https://www.scobserver.in/reports/supreme-court-rejects-plea-seeking-100-vote-verification-with-vvpats/))

**Against**

- **The Supreme Court examined it and made no finding of tampering** (stands). After questioning the ECI at length on the machines, the court dismissed the pleas and added further checks; no tampering has been shown.
  - In April 2024 the Supreme Court rejected all pleas for 100% VVPAT verification, including the suggestion to return to paper ballots, and made no finding of tampering. ([Supreme Court Observer, 2024-04-26](https://www.scobserver.in/reports/supreme-court-rejects-plea-seeking-100-vote-verification-with-vvpats/))
  - It ordered symbol loading units sealed for 45 days and allowed the second- or third-placed candidate to request a burnt-memory check of 5% of a seat's EVMs. ([Supreme Court Observer, 2024-04-26](https://www.scobserver.in/reports/supreme-court-rejects-plea-seeking-100-vote-verification-with-vvpats/))

**How they meet**

- "The Supreme Court examined it and made no finding of tampering" rebuts "Checks are too thin to catch tampering": the court examined the safeguards and found no shown instance
- "Checks are too thin to catch tampering" rebuts "The Supreme Court examined it and made no finding of tampering": a small sample can miss tampering
- *Judged stronger:* "The Supreme Court examined it and made no finding of tampering" over "Checks are too thin to catch tampering". A Supreme Court bench questioned the ECI at length on the machines and safeguards and made no finding of tampering; the petition argued that checks were too few, which is a case for more checking, not evidence that tampering happened. This is a judgment call, open to challenge.

### K9. The ECI enforces the Model Code of Conduct more softly against the ruling party.

*Meaning · contested*

**For**

- **The PM's speech drew a notice to his party, not to him** (open). When the complaint was about the Prime Minister's own words, the ECI wrote to the BJP president instead, a softer step.
  - On complaints about the Prime Minister's April 2024 Banswara speech, the ECI sent its notice to BJP president J P Nadda; the letter did not name Modi. ([Onmanorama, 2024-04-25](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/india/2024/04/25/election-commission-sends-notice-to-congress-bjp-over-hate-speech.amp.html))

**Against**

- **Both parties got the same treatment** (open). The ECI handled complaints against Congress leaders the same way, through their party president, and it was the first time it took cognisance of a complaint against any prime minister.
  - Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge received a similarly worded notice about complaints against him and Rahul Gandhi; the report says the Commission was holding party presidents responsible as a first step to rein in star campaigners. ([Onmanorama, 2024-04-25](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/india/2024/04/25/election-commission-sends-notice-to-congress-bjp-over-hate-speech.amp.html))
  - Officials quoted in the report said it was the first time the Commission had taken cognisance of a complaint against any prime minister. ([Onmanorama, 2024-04-25](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/india/2024/04/25/election-commission-sends-notice-to-congress-bjp-over-hate-speech.amp.html))

**How they meet**

- "Both parties got the same treatment" rebuts "The PM's speech drew a notice to his party, not to him": the same procedure was applied to both parties
- "The PM's speech drew a notice to his party, not to him" rebuts "Both parties got the same treatment": one procedural parallel does not show equal enforcement overall

### K10. Since late 2024, public access to election records and polling footage has been narrowed.

*Fact · supported*

**For**

- **A narrowed rule and a shorter retention period** (stands). The rule on which election papers the public may inspect was narrowed, and footage is now kept for a shorter time unless an election petition is filed.
  - In December 2024 Rule 93(2)(a) was amended, on the ECI's recommendation, so that only papers specified in the rules are open to public inspection, after a High Court ordered footage and documents released to a non-candidate. ([MediaNama, 2024-12-23](https://www.medianama.com/2024/12/223-centre-amends-election-rule-to-restrict-access-to-electronic-and-cctv-records/))
  - In June 2025 the ECI cut the retention of polling video and photos to 45 days, from three months to a year, citing misuse of clips by non-contestants; footage is kept longer where an election petition is filed, and 45 days matches the window for filing one. ([Scroll, 2025-06-20](https://scroll.in/latest/1083683/citing-misuse-ec-cuts-storage-time-of-polling-videos-photos-to-45-days))

### K11. Inside the ECI, the Chief Election Commissioner has taken decisions over the objections of the other two commissioners.

*Meaning · contested*

Also heard as: "One-man Election Commission"

**For**

- **Fourteen dissent notes on record** (open). If two of three commissioners repeatedly object on record, and say some decisions were issued without their knowledge, the CEC is deciding over their objections.
  - The Indian Express reported that Commissioners Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi recorded objections at least 14 times over ten months, on voter additions and deletions, Form 6, and control of the roll database. ### K12. The CEC and the Union government are acting together to tilt elections toward the ruling party.
